Process

Six Steps to
Homeownership

We handle the complexity. You focus on finding the home you love.

01

Choose Your Property

Select any new or existing home up to R1.5 million anywhere in South Africa. Work with our team to match your lifestyle and budget.

02

Submit Your Application

Complete our simple online form with your ID, payslips, bank statements, and proof of address. Affordability assessed at 30% of net income.

03

Offer & Agreement

We submit a formal offer to the seller on your behalf and draw up a comprehensive, transparent Rent-to-Buy agreement tailored to you.

04

Pay Your Deposit

Secure your home with a 10% refundable deposit. Funds are safely held via an attorneys' trust account for full legal compliance.

05

Move In

The property is registered in Sandton Property Capital's name during the rental period. You occupy your home from day one.

06

Receive Title Deed

After 15 years (or sooner with extra payments), the property is fully transferred to you or your designated next of kin.
